ABOUT THIS PREVIEW

Your email. A clear choice.

By Max Fisher · Draft for review · 2026-10-29

This is a private MBRACE INSIGHT website draft. The site helps DMV residents understand heat pumps, electrification incentives, and building-performance requirements. It records resource requests and newsletter preferences in a private database.

What is recorded

Your email address, request type, requested asset, signup source, supplied campaign tags, timestamp, and the consent wording/version are stored. Historical clearance requests, if submitted through a retained link, also store a public page URL and target query. Do not enter private signed links or credentials. Short-lived hashed request markers help limit abuse; raw network addresses are not stored in the contact list.

Resources and newsletter are separate

Entering your email for a resource records that request and gives you a download link. It does not request the newsletter unless you choose the separate checkbox. Standalone newsletter signup requires an explicit choice. Repeated requests use one contact record while retaining distinct request and consent events.

Confirmation and delivery

Newsletter requests remain pending email confirmation. This private preview does not send confirmation emails or newsletters automatically. An email delivery platform has not yet been connected. Download access is available immediately after a successful saved request.

Your preferences

A private preference link is shown after each successful request. Use it to withdraw newsletter signup or mark the address unsubscribed. Opening that link alone does not change preferences; the form requires a deliberate confirmation. For access, correction, or removal requests, contact mfisher@mbraceinsight.com.

Access and storage

The site uses Sites hosting with a private D1 database. Contact records and exports are restricted to the publisher. Test addresses ending in .invalid are excluded from the real-list exports. The hosting provider may process technical information to deliver and protect the site. External source sites have their own practices.

Before public launch

Final privacy terms, retention rules, the email confirmation/delivery workflow, editorial review, and the actual public page’s six-gate validation require completion. Subscriber data is not part of an unrestricted research or RAG corpus.

Get the next county finding.

Each issue of the Montgomery County edition leads with one finding from the public record, with its source and date. Newsletter signup is a separate choice from any download.
