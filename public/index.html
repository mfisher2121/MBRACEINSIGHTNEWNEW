A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Clear context.
Clear expectations.

Is this a lead list?+

No. The briefing reports counts and patterns from the county's published records. A “No” in a county projection does not show equipment condition, an owner's plans, a budget, or a project, and it is not a reason to contact a building.

Does “No” mean a building is out of compliance?+

No. “Meeting Interim Standard” is the county's published projection. Coverage, exemptions, alternative pathways, and deadlines are building-specific. Only the county can determine a building's compliance status.

Where does the data come from?+

The county's BEPS building inventory and energy performance map exports, joined by exact county building ID, plus the county's Mechanical Permits dataset for comparison. The briefing's source list gives each export's retrieval time and checksum.

Is this a government website?+

No. MBRACE INSIGHT is an independent publication. We link to the county's own tools so you can verify every figure. We do not claim government endorsement or privileged access.

Does a download subscribe me to the newsletter?+

Only if you select the separate, unchecked newsletter option. Download requests and newsletter requests are recorded separately. Use the preference link shown after signup to withdraw a newsletter request.

Will an email arrive?+

Not yet. This private preview records requests and unlocks downloads after saving. Email confirmation and newsletter delivery are not connected. Newsletter requests stay pending confirmation, and no email is sent automatically.
