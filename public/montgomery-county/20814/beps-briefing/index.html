MONTGOMERY COUNTY / 20814 / BEPS RECORDS

In 20814, the county marks 66 of 138 buildings “No.”

By Max Fisher · Draft for review · 2026-11-07

County exports retrieved November 7, 2026 · ZIP 20814 · Montgomery County, Maryland · Energy map reporting-year label, as published: 2026

The finding. Montgomery County’s energy performance map lists 66 of the 138 matched buildings in ZIP 20814 as not meeting their projected interim performance standard. 70 read Yes and 2 read Undisclosed. Hotels (6 of 6) and multifamily housing (33 of 52) account for most of the No readings. Offices run the other way: 40 of 50 read Yes.

What it can inform

The county publishes a projection for each building it tracks. Where one building type sits mostly on the “No” side, that type is where the county’s own figures put the most distance between published energy use and the interim standard. For an HVAC business that serves, or wants to serve, larger buildings in 20814, that makes it the building type to understand first: how those buildings are typically heated and cooled, who makes system decisions, and what the county’s rules ask of them.

The decision it can inform: which building type to study first, and which questions to bring to that conversation. It cannot tell you which building to call.

Against the projected final standard, the split widens: 79 read No, 57 read Yes, and 2 read Undisclosed.

How to read the columns

The map carries a “Benchmarking Report Status” column and separate “Meeting Interim Standard” and “Meeting Final Standard” columns. The first tracks whether a building’s benchmarking report was filed and accepted. The standard columns compare the building’s published energy use with its projected targets. 63 of the 66 buildings that read No for the interim standard read “in compliance” for benchmarking report status. Read each column on its own terms. Neither one is a compliance determination by MBRACE INSIGHT.

Coverage and gaps

  • 141 inventory records and 138 energy map records list postal code 20814. All 138 energy map records matched an inventory record by exact county building ID.
  • 3 inventory records have no energy map match: two multifamily buildings (named “Cecil” and “Hampden House” in the inventory) and one record with no name or use type.
  • Among matched records, 8 have a blank normalized net site EUI and 2 read “Undisclosed.” Both Undisclosed interim readings are multifamily buildings.
  • ZIP comes from the county’s published postal code. We did not assign ZIPs from street addresses. Two records elsewhere in the export carry invalid postal codes and are outside this scope.
  • The reporting-year label (2026) is the county’s. We have not mapped it to a calendar year of energy use.

What it does not show

Equipment type, age, or condition. An owner’s plans, budget, or timeline. Whether a building qualifies for an exemption or will pursue an alternative pathway. Any project, bid, or purchase. Confirm the building record and the county’s current requirements before relying on any value.

The permits comparison

The county’s Mechanical Permits dataset lists 559 permits with October 2026 issue dates; the latest issue date in the data is 2026-10-28, and the source last reported a row update on October 30. 23 list ZIP 20814: 18 replacements and 5 installs, covering 14 single-family dwellings, 2 townhouses, and 7 business buildings. That is recorded permitting activity, mostly residential, and a different population from the BEPS buildings above. City permit systems are outside the dataset, and a permit does not confirm the equipment type or completed work.

Method

  1. Downloaded the county BEPS building inventory and the energy performance map with their official export controls on November 7, 2026, and recorded each file’s SHA-256 checksum.
  2. Kept records whose published postal code is 20814.
  3. Joined the two exports on exact county building ID. No name-based or fuzzy matching.
  4. Kept every published value as text, including blanks and “Undisclosed,” and counted directly from those values.
  5. Queried the county Mechanical Permits API for October issue dates and confirmed the download matched an independent count query. A recheck on November 7 returned the same 559 records.
