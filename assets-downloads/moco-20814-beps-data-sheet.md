## What this is

A building-level table of what Montgomery County publishes for ZIP 20814: the county BEPS building inventory joined to the county energy performance map by exact county building ID. Field values are copied as published. MBRACE INSIGHT has not changed, estimated, or filled in any value.

## What this is not

- Not a compliance determination. The “Meeting Interim Standard” and “Meeting Final Standard” columns are the county's projections, as published.
- Not a lead list. A “No” does not show equipment age or condition, an owner's plans, a budget, or a project.
- Not complete coverage. Buildings outside the county BEPS inventory, and ZIP assignments the county did not publish as 20814, are outside this table.

## Method

1. Downloaded the county building inventory and energy map exports with their official export controls on November 7, 2026.
2. Kept records whose published postal code is 20814. Postal codes were not inferred from addresses.
3. Joined the two exports on exact county building ID. No fuzzy or name-based matching.
4. Kept every published value as text, including blanks and “Undisclosed.” Flagged 31 review items in the collector's queue (invalid postal codes outside this scope, missing matches, and missing or undisclosed measurements).
